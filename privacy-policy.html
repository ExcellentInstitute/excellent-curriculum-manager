PART 1: PRIVACY POLICY

1. Introduction

Welcome to Odisha Books 1-10 & AI Tutor ("the App"), developed and maintained by Excellent Institute, Odisha, India ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of students, parents, teachers, and all users—especially children in Classes 1 to 10—in accordance with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable Indian data protection laws (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

2. Non-Government Affiliation Disclaimer

This App is an independent, private educational application by Excellent Institute. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to the Government of Odisha, OSEPA, BSE Odisha, SCERT, or NCERT. Publicly available textbook references are sourced from official public portals such as OSEPA and BSE Odisha strictly for educational convenience.

3. No Account Required & Zero Personal Data Collection

  • We do NOT require students to create an account, sign in, or submit personal information (such as full name, phone number, address, school name, date of birth, or personal photos).
  • All study preferences—selected class, bookmarked pages, last-read page, Light/Dark theme, Odia/English UI language, study streak, stars, and notification mute setting—are stored strictly locally on the user's device.

4. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO) & Voice Questions

  • Optional Microphone permission is requested strictly when a user holds the Push-to-Talk microphone button or explicitly turns on the "Hi Foxy" hands-free voice toggle to ask a textbook study question.
  • Voice audio clips are sent over encrypted HTTPS solely to answer the study question and are deleted immediately from device cache after processing.
  • We do not record audio in the background without user activation, and we never store, sell, or share voice recordings for advertising or profiling.

5. Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) & Quiet Hours

  • On Android 13+, the App requests optional Notification permission to deliver Live Class alerts, newly uploaded video lessons, Notice Board updates, and class-wise Exam Papers.
  • Notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) using anonymous topic subscriptions (all_students and class_1–class_10) with zero personal user identifiers.
  • Standard alerts follow Indian Standard Time (IST) Quiet Hours (9:00 PM – 7:00 AM IST). Users or parents can mute notifications anytime inside the App's Settings menu or Android Settings.

6. Foxy AI Tutor & Automated Child-Safety Shield

  • Study question text, optional textbook page images, or temporary spoken questions are routed securely over encrypted HTTPS through our private Cloudflare Worker proxy to AI providers to generate explanations and voice narration.
  • Automated child-safety filters check both user input and AI output to block inappropriate, vulgar, or harmful content.
  • Anonymized, non-personal educational page explanations (zero personal identifiers) may be cached to load answers faster and reduce student mobile data usage.

7. Children's Privacy, Parental Gate & Child-Directed Ads (Google AdMob)

  • All Google AdMob ads are strictly configured with Child-Directed Treatment enabled (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE) and Maximum Ad Content Rating set to G (General Audiences).
  • Personalized / interest-based advertising and behavioral remarketing are completely disabled.
  • Parental Gate: External links (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps), public YouTube Live Chat, and Google Play purchases/subscriptions are locked behind an adult verification prompt.
  • Optional Student Pro Pass or AI Super Tutor plans remove all advertisements inside the App.

  • The optional Odia Smart Dictionary is stored locally in an on-device SQLite database for offline search.
  • Exam Routine & Question Papers (formatted text, question images, or whitelisted HTML links) and custom web sections restrict navigation strictly to trusted educational domains.
  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

10. Data Deletion & Parental Rights

Because we store no personal student accounts on our servers, parents or users can delete all locally saved app data (bookmarks, streaks, cached books, and offline dictionary) anytime via Android Settings → Clear Storage or by uninstalling the App.
